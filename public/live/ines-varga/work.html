Selected maps, 2022–2025

Four maps, newest first. Each one was walked or read before a line went down.

start here

  1. Book map, 2025

    The Salt Isles

    Endpaper map for a seafaring novel, drawn from the author’s notes and a box of sketches.

    An archipelago that doesn’t exist

  2. Town map, 2024

    Centro on Foot

    A folding walking map of the old centre, with the trees, the avenues and the shortcuts locals use.

    A city centre, walked street by street

  3. Neighbourhood map, 2023

    Riverside Quarter

    Every building in fine line, for the residents who wanted their streets on one wall.

    A quarter between the river and the rail line

  4. Trail map, 2022

    The Lake Loop

    A trail map for the path around the lake, with every bench, spring and steep bit on it.

    A 14 km loop around a town lake
