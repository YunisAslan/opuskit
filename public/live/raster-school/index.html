How to get a seat

From application to your first evening

  1. 1

    Step 1: Apply

    Tell us who you are, which cohort you want and what you would like to make a poster about.

    10 minutes

  2. 2

    Step 2: A short call

    We call to check the course fits you. No portfolio needed, no test.

    15 minutes

  3. 3

    Step 3: Hold your seat

    A deposit of CHF 200 holds one of the twelve seats. The rest is due two weeks before the first evening.

    Within a week

  4. 4

    Step 4: Week zero

    A reading list, the kit list and a first small exercise arrive a week before you start.

    1 week
