Raster School

Six weeks,week by week

Twelve evenings, Tue and Thu 18:30–21:30. Each week adds one layer to the same poster, so by week six you are not starting, you are printing.

  1. Week 1The baseline
  2. Week 2Columns
  3. Week 3Modules
  4. Week 4Letters
  5. Week 5The poster
  6. Week 6Print

  1. 1Week 1

    The baseline

    How text is measured, and how a page gets its rhythm from one number.

    • Reading a type specimen
    • Measure and leading, by eye and by number
    • Setting a baseline grid
    • One page set three ways

    Hand in

    A text page on a 12 pt baseline

  2. 2Week 2

    Columns

    Margins, columns and gutters: the frame every later decision sits in.

    • Margins and proportion
    • Columns, gutters and fields
    • Hierarchy by size and position
    • Flush left, and when not to

    Hand in

    An event programme on 12 columns

  3. 3Week 3

    Modules

    Fields across rows and columns, and how pictures and tables live in them.

    • Fields and modules
    • Cropping pictures to the grid
    • Tables and timetables
    • Rules that sit on the lines

    Hand in

    A four-page leaflet

  4. 4Week 4

    Letters

    Drawing letters by hand, on a grid, until a word holds together.

    • Constructing letters on a grid
    • Stroke, contrast and width
    • Spacing a word by eye
    • From pencil to outline

    Hand in

    Your poster’s headline, drawn

  5. 5Week 5

    The poster

    Your own subject, three layouts, one decision.

    • Choosing a subject
    • Three layouts in one evening
    • Crit with the whole cohort
    • One signal colour

    Hand in

    The final layout, at full size

  6. 6Week 6

    Print

    Proofs at 1:1, print day in the studio and a last crit in front of the wall.

    • Proofing at full size
    • Preparing files for Riso
    • Print day, Saturday
    • Final crit and hanging

    You keep

    50 copies of your poster, A2

Every lecture is recorded. Online cohorts follow the same weeks, on the same evenings.

How an evening runs

Tuesday and Thursday, 18:30 to 21:30

  1. 1

    Step 1: Look

    Ten posters on the wall and one question about them. We start by looking, not talking.

    15 minutes

  2. 2

    Step 2: Lecture

    One idea, shown in real work, with the grid drawn over it.

    30 minutes

  3. 3

    Step 3: Draw

    Studio time with pencil, ruler and screen. The instructors go from desk to desk.

    90 minutes

  4. 4

    Step 4: Pin-up

    Everyone pins the evening’s sheet to the wall. We talk about three of them, out loud.

    30 minutes
