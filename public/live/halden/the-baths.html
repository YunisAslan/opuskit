The baths

One wood-fired room, one cold sea, and the time it takes to move between them.

What is here

  • The sauna

    A wood-fired room for twelve, birch on the stove, the sea through one long window.

  • The sea

    A ladder off the jetty into open water, every month of the year.

  • Smoke sauna

    Heated for six hours, aired, then entered. Saturdays, from October to April.

  • Cedar tubs

    Two tubs on the rocks, fired by hand, for the time between dips.

  • The quiet room

    Wool blankets, tea from the stove, no phones past the door.

  • Private evenings

    The whole bathhouse for your group, after eight.

The round

  1. 1

    Step 1: Arrive

    Change, shower, leave the phone in the locker. The staff walk you through the house once.

    15 min

  2. 2

    Step 2: Heat

    Sit high for heat, low for less. Water on the stones whenever the room agrees.

    10–15 min

  3. 3

    Step 3: Cold

    The jetty ladder, or the cold tub if the sea is rough. Breathe out on the way down.

    1–2 min

  4. 4

    Step 4: Rest

    Blanket, tea, the window. Most people go round three times.

    20 min
