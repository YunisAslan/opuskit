The longquiet

What is here

  • The sauna

    A wood-fired room for twelve, birch on the stove, the sea through one long window.

  • The sea

    A ladder off the jetty into open water, every month of the year.

  • Smoke sauna

    Heated for six hours, aired, then entered. Saturdays, from October to April.

  • Cedar tubs

    Two tubs on the rocks, fired by hand, for the time between dips.

  • The quiet room

    Wool blankets, tea from the stove, no phones past the door.

  • Private evenings

    The whole bathhouse for your group, after eight.

A session, start to end

  1. Step 1

    Book a time

    Two hours, chosen online; arrive a quarter of an hour early.

  2. Step 2

    Heat

    Ten minutes on the cedar benches, or as long as it feels right.

  3. Step 3

    The sea

    Down the jetty ladder, under once, and out again.

  4. Step 4

    Rest

    By the stove in wool, until the body comes back. Then again.

The house

Built on the rocks

Bare timber on the rocks, the sea on three sides, the stove lit before first light.

Birch and stone

The fire is fed by hand all day. You can hear it from the benches.

Eight degrees

The water in February. Nobody stays in long; everybody goes back.

After

Wool to the chin, salt on the lips, nothing to say.

Said on the way out
