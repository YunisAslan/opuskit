The boat shed

Kür Delta Watch Boat shed 3, Liman street Neftchala AZ1700, Azerbaijan
  • Cleanups: Saturdays 08:00 to 13:00, April to November
  • Office: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 10:00 to 17:00

From Baku, take the Neftchala bus from the main bus station (about 2 hours 40 minutes). The shed is a ten-minute walk from the last stop, past the harbour.
