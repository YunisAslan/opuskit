What we do

The work

Five things, done the same way every time, so the numbers can be compared year on year.

Our work

  • Cleanup Saturdays

    Every Saturday from April to November, on the banks and in the reeds between the bridge and the sea. Every sack is weighed before it goes in the skip.

  • The monthly water test

    Six fixed sampling points on the first Sunday of the month. A lab in Baku measures oxygen, nitrates, phosphates, oil products and five more; we publish all of it.

  • Boat days

    Two boats reach the side channels you can’t walk to, where most of the rubbish ends up. A good boat day brings out half a tonne.

  • Bird counts

    Spring and autumn, on the same route by boat, with Gulnara Sadigova who has counted here since the 1980s. 63 species last time.

  • School visits

    Forty minutes in a classroom, then an afternoon on the bank with gloves and a scale. Twenty-three schools so far.

A cleanup Saturday

  1. 1

    Sign in at the boat shed

    Gloves, sacks, a grabber and waders if you need them. Bring water and boots you don’t mind ruining.

    08:00, 15 minutes

  2. 2

    The briefing

    Which stretch, where the boats go, and what not to touch: needles and gas canisters get flagged, and we call the municipality.

    15 minutes

  3. 3

    Bag it

    Teams of four work along the bank and into the reeds. The boats take the channels.

    3 hours

  4. 4

    Weigh it

    Every sack is hung on the scale and written in the logbook before it goes in the skip.

    30 minutes

  5. 5

    Tea and the number

    The day’s total is read out before anyone goes home.

    20 minutes
