Lowfield Nights, 12–14 June 2027

Three nights of silent films with live scores, in a hangar the airfield forgot.

Each night has one film and one score written for this room, then a late screening outside on the apron. Entry is free with an RSVP: 220 seats a night, on benches and blankets. The sea is two minutes’ walk from the door.

One film a night,scored in the room.

The programme

Three nights. Doors at 19:00, the film at 20:30.

The nights, hour by hour

Saturday 12 June

  1. 19:00

    Doors and the long table

    Bread, cheese and tea on the apron while the light goes.

  2. 20:30

    Sevil (1929)

    Amo Bek-Nazarov’s silent drama, scored live by Kamran Sadiq on tar and tape loops.

  3. 22:40

    Night set on the apron

    Kamran Sadiq and guests, outside, until the last shuttle.

Sunday 13 June

  1. 19:00

    Doors and the long table

    Tea, bread and the food stalls open.

  2. 20:30

    Man with a Movie Camera (1929)

    Dziga Vertov’s city symphony with Tural Amirli’s double bass quartet.

  3. 22:30

    Sunrise (1927), under the sky

    Murnau on the outdoor screen, Ines Varga at the piano and electronics.

Monday 14 June

  1. 19:00

    Doors and the long table

    The last long table. Stay for the walk.

  2. 20:30

    The Passion of Joan of Arc (1928)

    Dreyer’s close-ups, a choir of twelve, Nora Halloway on voice and strings.

  3. 22:30

    Last light

    We walk to the shore together. Bring a layer.

The players

Each score is written for this hangar and played once.

Who plays when

Who plays, and when

  • Kamran Sadiq

    Tar and tape loops, Saturday

    Sevil was shot two streets from where I grew up. I score it from memory.

  • Tural Amirli

    Double bass, Sunday

    I play Vertov like a train timetable: on the beat, then just off it.

  • Ines Varga

    Piano and electronics, Sunday late

    Sunrise is a love story told mostly in fog. The piano should sound like fog.

  • Nora Halloway

    Voice and strings, Monday

    Joan barely speaks. The choir does the speaking for her.

The light at the endis the coast.
