How a batch is made, from the lake to your shelf

  1. 1

    Rake

    Salt from Masazir, raked by hand in August, dried on the lab roof for a week and washed twice in lake water.

  2. 2

    Steep

    Saffron from Bilgah sits in squalane for six weeks, until the oil turns the colour of late afternoon.

  3. 3

    Pour

    Nigar mixes each batch in the lab in Mardakan and pours it in one day, 300 bottles at a time.

  4. 4

    Number

    Every bottle is labelled by hand with its batch and the day it was poured, then rests a week before it leaves.
