The KOFİİ team behind the counter

We opened KOFİİ because we wanted a coffee shop where you can watch your drink being made, start to finish.

The same small team runs the bar every day. We roast with a local partner, bake in the shop each morning, and keep the menu short enough to make everything well.

No loyalty app, no queue system. Say hello, tell us how you like it, and we will remember next time.

How we got here

  1. The first cart

    KOFİİ started as a two-group espresso cart at the Saturday market. One grinder, one menu board.

  2. A room with a window

    We moved into a small room on Birch Street. Six tables, a long bench and a lot of daylight.

  3. Baking in the shop

    Cheesecake and cardamom buns joined the menu, baked each morning before we open.

  4. Cold drinks, done properly

    We added cold brew, frappés and matcha, made with the same care as a hot flat white.

  5. Today

    Two locations, the same small team, and the same rule: every drink is made to order.
