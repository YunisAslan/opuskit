Do I need a reservation?+

No. Most tables are walk-in. Book ahead for groups of five or more, or for weekend mornings.

Do you have plant-based milk?+

Yes. Oat and almond are always on, at no extra cost.

Can I bring my laptop?+

Yes, on weekdays. There are plugs along the window bench. On weekends we keep tables free for people eating.

Is the shop step-free?+

Yes. The entrance is level, and the toilet is accessible.

Are dogs welcome?+

Yes. We keep water bowls by the door.

Do you sell beans to take home?+

Yes. We grind them for your brewer if you ask, or sell them whole.

Can I order ahead for pickup?+

Yes. Order online and choose a pickup time. We start the drink when you are five minutes away.
