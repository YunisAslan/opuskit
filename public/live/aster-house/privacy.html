Privacy

This site stores nothing about you. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

The viewing form does not send anything itself: it opens your own mail app with a message to viewings@asterhouse.az. What you send there reaches the sales office, who use it only to arrange your viewing and to reply to you.

We keep viewing emails for twelve months after the last houses are handed over, then delete them. To see or delete what we hold about you, write to viewings@asterhouse.az.
