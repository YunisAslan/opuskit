In every house

  • Cut into the rock

    The back half of every house is inside the cliff: sixty centimetres of limestone keep the rooms cool in August and warm in January.

  • The hour room

    One room per house, set at the angle of its hour, with a wall left bare so the light has something to land on.

  • A terrace at the water

    A private stone terrace at sea level, between 34 and 71 square metres, with a plunge pool fed with filtered sea water.

  • The stair through the cliff

    One concrete stair runs from the living floor at the top down through the rock to the sea rooms.

  • Heat from the sea

    A shared sea-water heat pump warms the floors and the pools. Nothing burns on site.

  • Quiet by design

    Twelve houses and no more. No shared pool deck, no restaurant, no visitors’ car park on the cliff.
