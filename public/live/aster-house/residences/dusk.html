Inside Dusk

  • Dusk, the living floor

    The top floor is one room: kitchen, table and sofa under the cliff edge, with the sea filling the long window.

  • Going down

    The stair is cut into the rock, lit from one slot above, and ends at the sea rooms.

  • From the water

    Dusk is house 10 of twelve, counted from the east along the rock.

Three floors

  • At 19:30

    The hour room

    One deep window, and the last red light laid across the floor of the sitting room. The wall opposite the window is left bare, in the cliff’s own limestone.

  • 2 bedrooms

    The sea rooms

    Bedrooms sit at the bottom of the house, at the level of the water, each with a window cut at an angle to the sea and its own bathroom.

  • 40 m²

    The terrace

    A private stone terrace with a plunge pool fed with filtered sea water. Steps lead down to the rocks for a swim in the morning.
