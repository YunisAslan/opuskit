About Brasshand

Three people, one room on Rasul Rza street, and a habit of naming things before drawing them.

Leyla started Brasshand in 2017 after ten years of writing ads she didn’t believe in. Rauf joined to draw the letters, Nigar to get them printed on time. We work for restaurants, labels, festivals and anyone in Baku who opens a door and wants people to walk through it.
