01FAQ

Questions worth asking.

  • 01What do you actually make?+−

    Art direction for the web: the type system, the colour, how media is cropped and how things move. We design and build, so the idea survives into the browser.

  • 02Is all of this 3D going to make my site slow?+−

    No. One real-time scene per page, lazy-loaded, paused when off-screen. Phones and reduced-motion settings get a still image. We test against real Core Web Vitals targets, not a fast laptop.

  • 03Can people still find their way around?+−

    Yes. We break the grid on purpose, never the navigation. Menus stay where people expect them, and every section keeps one clear anchor.

  • 04How long does a project take?+−

    Most identity-plus-site projects run six to ten weeks. Smaller experiments — a campaign page, a single interactive piece — can take two to three.

  • 05Do you work with our existing brand?+−

    Often. We push an existing identity further rather than replacing it, unless the brief is to start fresh.

  • 06What does it cost?+−

    Every project is scoped individually. Tell us what you want to make and when; we reply with a fixed price and a plan within a week.
