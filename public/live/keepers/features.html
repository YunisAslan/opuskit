What is in the can

Most canned coffee is brewed hot, cooled and sweetened to hide it. Keepers is brewed cold, zested by hand and carbonated gently. Here is what that means for the drink.

  1. Cold brew, not instant

    Coarse-ground arabica steeps in 4°C water for 18 hours. Cold extraction pulls out about 60% less acid than hot brewing, so the coffee tastes of chocolate and stone fruit rather than char.

    pH 5.1, measured per batch

  2. Real peel, no flavouring

    We zest Sicilian oranges and Amalfi lemons and steep the peel with the coffee. The oils carry the citrus, so there is no added flavouring and no citric acid on the label.

    2.4 g of peel in every can

  3. Fine bubbles that last

    Carbonated to 2.8 volumes, a little softer than tonic. The bubbles stay small, so the drink feels bright without the bloat of a cola.

    2.8 volumes CO₂

  4. A can that comes back

    Aluminium is recycled more often than any other drinks packaging in Europe. Ours starts at 68% recycled content and carries no plastic ring or shrink wrap.

    68% recycled aluminium
