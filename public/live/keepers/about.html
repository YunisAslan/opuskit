Hackney Wick, since 2023

Keepers started because we wanted an afternoon coffee that was cold, fizzy and not a sugar bomb. Nobody made one we liked, so we spent a year making eleven versions in a shared kitchen. Number eleven is in the can.

Marco Ferri

Co-founder, coffee

Marco spent nine years roasting for speciality cafés in London. He built the cold-brew rig in our unit from two brewery fermenters and a pond pump, and still tastes every batch before it is canned.

Lena Aydin

Co-founder, drinks

Lena ran the bar at a Hackney wine shop and spent two summers on the Amalfi coast learning to cure citrus. She wrote the recipe that became Keepers on the back of a delivery note in 2023.

“We make one drink. If we ever make a second, it will be because the first one is finished.”

Lena Aydin
