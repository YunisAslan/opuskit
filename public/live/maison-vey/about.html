About

We are two people at one bench in Sète, and we only make what we have stood inside.

Hélène Vey trained as a perfumer in Grasse and spent eleven years composing for larger houses. Tomas Vey is a carpenter who built the workshop, its shelves and the crates the bottles travel in. They opened Maison Vey in 2019 above a chandlery on the quai de la Marine, with one scent and a list of four more places they wanted to keep.

From the place to your shelf

  1. Step 1

    Standing in the place

    We return to the same place at the same hour, again and again, and write down what is really there.

    One season

  2. Step 2

    Building the formula

    Hélène sketches the scent at the bench and wears each draft for a week before changing one thing.

    Six months to two years

  3. Step 3

    Resting

    Each batch of two hundred is mixed by hand and left in glass, in the dark, to round off.

    Six weeks

  4. Step 4

    Filling and numbering

    Filtered cold, poured by hand, stoppered, labelled and numbered by whoever filled it.

    Two days a batch

  5. Step 5

    Sent to you

    Folded into a box Tomas designed, with a sample of another place tucked beside it.

    Two working days
