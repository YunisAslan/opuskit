Treatment,then the mat

Every course at Hane has two halves: hands-on work in the room, and a few slow exercises you take home. The first makes things easier; the second keeps them that way.

What we treat

  • First visit

    An hour to talk, test how you move and agree a plan in plain words.

  • Hands-on treatment

    Slow, specific manual therapy for backs, necks, shoulders, hips and knees.

  • Slow-movement sessions

    One to one on the mat: the exercises, taught until they feel like yours.

  • After surgery or injury

    A steady plan back to walking, lifting, running or sleeping well.

  • Your home programme

    A short written set of exercises after every visit, changed as you improve.

How a course goes

  1. 1

    First visit

    We take your history, watch how you move and test what hurts. You leave with a plan in plain words.

    60 minutes

  2. 2

    Treatment

    Hands-on work on the problem, then we check your exercises together so they are done right.

    45 minutes, weekly at first

  3. 3

    At home

    Ten minutes a day from your home programme. We change it at each visit as things get easier.

    10 minutes a day

  4. 4

    Check-in

    We retest, look back at the plan and decide together whether you still need us.

    30 minutes, after about six weeks
