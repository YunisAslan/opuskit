What we treat

  • First visit

    An hour to talk, test how you move and agree a plan in plain words.

  • Hands-on treatment

    Slow, specific manual therapy for backs, necks, shoulders, hips and knees.

  • Slow-movement sessions

    One to one on the mat: the exercises, taught until they feel like yours.

  • After surgery or injury

    A steady plan back to walking, lifting, running or sleeping well.

  • Your home programme

    A short written set of exercises after every visit, changed as you improve.

What happenswhen you come in

Most courses are three to six visits over about six weeks. Each one has the same shape: we listen, we treat, and you leave with something to do at home.

  1. Step 1 of 3

    We listen

    Your first hour is mostly talking and testing: where it hurts, when, and what you want to be able to do again.

  2. Step 2 of 3

    We treat

    Hands-on work, slow and specific, to ease what is stiff or sore so that movement can come back.

  3. Step 3 of 3

    You keep it going

    Two or three exercises, ten minutes a day on your own floor. That is the part that stops it coming back.
