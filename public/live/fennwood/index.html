Larkrise Farm grows our vegetables and Stonepit our grain, apples and cider, both under an hour away. Bread goes into the oven at dawn, vegetables follow while the bricks are hottest, and by evening the fire has settled for fish and slow lamb.

  • Embered vegetables£12
