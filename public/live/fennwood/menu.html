From the oven first

  • Oven bread£5

    Baked at dawn in the same oven, with cultured butter and flaky salt.

  • Embered vegetables£12

    Peppers, courgette, carrot and red onion from Larkrise Farm, whipped feta, burnt honey.

  • Leeks in the ashes£10

    Cooked black in the embers, peeled, with mustard dressing and a soft egg.

  • Smoked beetroot£9

    Fresh curd, toasted hazelnut, the beet leaves wilted in brown butter.

Larger plates

  • Whole fish from the grill£28

    The day’s catch from Brixham, lemon, charred courgette and new potatoes.

  • Lamb shoulder£26

    In the oven overnight as the fire dies, with white beans and salsa verde.

  • Roast squash£19

    Brown butter, sage and spelt from Stonepit Farm, a spoon of chilli oil.

Afters

  • Baked apple£8

    Oat crumble and cold pouring cream.

  • Burnt honey custard£8

    Set in the last heat of the oven.

  • Cheese£11

    One West Country cheese, oatcakes, quince.

To drink

  • Cider from Stonepit Farm£6

    Dry and still, by the glass or the bottle.

  • House wine£8

    A red and a white from a small grower in the Loire.

  • Rhubarb soda£4

    Made in the kitchen, not too sweet.
