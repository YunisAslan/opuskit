// 01 About// 01 About

Point of view

Most people who grade learn alone, from tutorials, and never sit in on a working session. Night Shift is that session.

Hanne started as an assistant at a post house in 2009 and has graded freelance since 2014: the cold night exteriors of Northern Line, the low sun of The Salt Year, commercials that need skin to look like skin. She started the course in 2023, between jobs, and still grades by day.
