// 02 One shot// 02 One shot

One shot, before and after

The golden-hour shot on the console above, as the camera recorded it and as it leaves the suite. Drag the line.

Week
5, Skin
In the suite

Nodes

The problem: log keeps the sun in the sky and the detail in her hair, and leaves everything grey. Backlit, her face sits two stops under the field.

The approach: balance on the scopes first. Gamma lifts the face, a soft window holds the sky, and a skin key puts her on the vectorscope’s skin line instead of guessing by eye.

The result: warm light that belongs to the evening, a sky that keeps its detail, and skin that reads as skin. Students grade this shot in week 5.

How a week runs

  1. 1

    Tuesday session

    The week’s technique, taught live on the week’s shot. My grade is on screen the whole time; ask anything.

    Tue 19:00–21:30

  2. 2

    Your grade

    Grade the shot on your own machine. Footage, references and a node template are in the course folder.

    3–5 hours

  3. 3

    Thursday review

    Your grade on the big screen, with notes from me and the cohort. Twelve people means every grade is reviewed.

    Thu 19:00–21:30

  4. 4

    Hand in

    Export and upload by Sunday night. Written notes come back to you by Tuesday.

    Sun 23:59

// 04 Instructor// 04 Instructor

Who teaches

I teach colour the way I learnt it: on real shots, with someone looking over your shoulder.

Hanne Vik is a colourist in Oslo. She graded The Salt Year, Low Tide Hotel and both seasons of Northern Line, and has run Night Shift since 2023. She still grades by day, which is why the course runs in the evening.
