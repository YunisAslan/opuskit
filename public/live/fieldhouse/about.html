The studio

A small studio for old barns, and only for old barnsA small studiofor old barns,and only forold barnsA small studio forold barns, and onlyfor old barns

Clara Wren and Owen Hale started Fieldhouse in 2011, after ten years repairing farm buildings for the National Trust. We work from a cart shed in Herefordshire and take on four or five barns a year, so that one of us is on every site, every week.

How a barn becomes a houseHow a barn becomes a house

  1. 1One day

    Walk the barn

    We spend a day with you on site, looking at what is sound, what is worth keeping and where the light falls.

  2. 2Three weeks

    Survey and record

    Every timber, joint and stone is measured and drawn, so nothing is lost by accident.

  3. 3Four to six months

    Drawings and permission

    We design the house inside the barn and take it through planning and listed building consent.

  4. 4Nine to fourteen months

    Build with the makers

    We choose the builder with you and stay on site every week until the last board is fixed.

  5. 5One day, and a year on

    Hand over the keys

    A walk round the finished house, a book of every joint we kept, and a visit a year later.
