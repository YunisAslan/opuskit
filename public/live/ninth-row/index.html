The cinema

A hundred and twenty seats, one screen, a film every night.

Ninth Row shows new arthouse films in their first week and old ones on the best print we can find. On Fridays a late-night series runs past midnight. Every screening has tickets, and every ticket has a seat.

Seasons

What runs through the programme, week after week.

  • Late-night series, since 2019

    Friday Late

    Every Friday, one film after 23:00. Cult, horror, the odd forgotten noir. The bar stays open until the credits end.

    See the week

  • Old films, since 2017

    Second Run

    Films from before most of us were born, on the best print we can find. Restorations when they exist, 35mm when they don’t.

    See the week

  • New films, every week

    First Week

    New arthouse releases in their first seven days, shown the way their makers meant them to be seen.

    See the week

  • Afternoons, since 2021

    Sunday Matinee

    A three o’clock film for anyone who would rather be home by dark. Children welcome.

    See the week
