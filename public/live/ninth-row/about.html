Who keeps the lamp lit

We sat in the ninth row of an old picture house for ten years, until it closed. So we built another one.

Ninth Row opened in 2017 in a former print works off Lantern Yard. Mara Okafor programmes the films; Tomas Reyes runs the booth, which still has a working 35mm projector next to the digital one. A hundred and twenty seats, one screen, a film every night since.
