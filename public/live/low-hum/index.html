The bar

A listening bar for people who would rather hear the record than shout over it.

The shelves hold ten thousand records. The speakers were built by hand, in this room, for this room. The kitchen sends out small plates until late. Come for one side of an album and stay for both.

Something is always on the platter. Here is roughly what, and when.

Tuesday to Thursday

  1. 17:00

    Doors, first record

    Whatever the bartender pulled from the shelf on the way in.

  2. 19:00

    Album hour

    One record, both sides, start to finish. Talk stays at a hum.

  3. 21:00

    Selector’s choice

    The staff pick from the ten thousand. No skips.

  4. 23:30

    Last plates

    The kitchen calls it. The records keep going till midnight.

Friday and Saturday

  1. 17:00

    Doors, first record

    Early drinks, early plates, plenty of room.

  2. 20:00

    Guest selector

    A collector brings a crate and plays it through our system.

  3. 23:00

    Late listening

    Lights down, volume just right, small plates still coming.

  4. 01:00

    Last plates

    Cheese on toast o’clock. Lights up at 2.

Sunday

  1. 15:00

    Sunday sides

    Jazz, folk and slow soul with a long, lazy menu.

  2. 18:00

    Request hour

    Write a record on a napkin. If it is on the shelf, it gets played.

  3. 22:30

    Last plates

    One more side, then home. We close at 11.
