Commission a book

Four books from the bakery table

Each one started as a pencil drawing at the same long table. Scroll slowly: every book brings its own colours with it.

  • Larkmoor Books, 2019

    The Loudest Rooster in Sheki

    A rooster who crows before the sun is up learns that the town already keeps its own time: church bells, bread ovens and the silk mill whistle.

  • Tidewell Press, 2021

    Nine Koi and a Copper Moon

    A counting book that sinks slowly: nine fish, one moon in the pond, and a heron the reader spots long before the koi do.

  • Fenlark Editions, 2023

    Sparrow Keeps the Spring

    Through a cold March a sparrow guards the magnolia buds. Nobody thanks her. She does it anyway, and the tree knows.

  • Hollin & Pike, 2016

    The Moths Who Came to Tea

    Twelve moths follow a lamp into a grandmother’s kitchen and stay for tea. The first book, and the one the studio is named after.
