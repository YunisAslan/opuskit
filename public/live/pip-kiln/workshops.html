What happenswhen

Saturday

  1. 10:00

    Wedge it

    Kettle on, aprons on. Squish the air out of your clay.

  2. 10:30

    Throw it

    Centre the lump on the wheel and pull up your first walls.

  3. 13:00

    Lunch

    Soup in our mugs, obviously. Your pots firm up meanwhile.

  4. 14:00

    Handle it

    Trim the base and pull a handle. This is where it becomes a mug.

  5. 15:30

    Pick a glaze

    Choose Sunshine, Raspberry or Liquorice. We do the dipping.

Three weeks later

  1. Week 1

    Bisque firing

    Your piece dries slowly, then gets its first trip to the kiln.

  2. Week 3

    Glaze firing

    Second firing at 1,240°C. The colour wakes up.

  3. Any Sat

    Collect it

    Pick it up from the studio, or we post it for £6.
