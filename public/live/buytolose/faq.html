ASKFrequently asked questions

The questions we get most before people buy.
Will the fleece shrink?+
It is garment-dyed and pre-washed, so it has already shrunk as much as it will. Wash cold, dry flat, and it stays the size you bought.
What if my size sells out?+
Each run is two hundred pieces and we do not restock the same colour. Sign up and we email you the day the next run opens.
How long does delivery take?+
1 to 2 working days in Portugal, 2 to 5 across the EU. Full details on shipping and returns.
Can I return something?+
Yes, within 30 days if it is unworn with tags. EU returns are free. Gift cards and crisps are final sale.
Where is it made?+
Everything we sew is made by two workshops in Porto, a ten-minute walk from our studio. The cups and crisps come from partners in Portugal and Spain.
How should I choose between two sizes?+
Take the larger for the hoodie if you want it loose, the smaller for the sweatpant. The size guide has flat measurements.
Do you ship outside Europe?+
Yes, to the UK, US, Canada, Japan and Australia. Import duties are paid by the recipient on delivery.
