About Slow Atlas

Three editors who pay their own way and stay until a place stops performing for them.

Slow Atlas started in 2023 at a kitchen table in Bergen, after Marit Lund left the travel desk of a daily paper. Three editors run it today. There are no sponsored trips, no affiliate links and no lists.

The editors

  • Marit Lund

    Editor

    I commission one place at a time and say no to most pitches.

  • Daniel Okafor

    Senior writer

    I take the slowest transport available and write down what happens.

  • Ines Varga

    Writer and picture editor

    I choose photographs that show the place, not the person who went there.
