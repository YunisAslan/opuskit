Sticky Weather is three people at one long table in Bristol.

Small studio? Big drawer of paper.Small studio? Big drawer of paper.

Mara started Sticky Weather in 2019 after ten years of packaging nobody kept. Rio joined to fold things, Arjun to make them move. We work with small brands that would rather be loved than everywhere: cafés, shops, makers and the odd paper mill. No account managers, no decks for decks. You talk to the people drawing.

Hover a face. It peels a little.

Small team? Big opinions.

  • Mara Lindqvist

  • Arjun Mehta

  2. 2

  4. 4
