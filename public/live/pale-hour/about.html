About

We found the building empty, the last press still bolted to the floor, and decided to keep it that way.

Hana Lindqvist curated for twelve years in Stockholm and London; Owen Marsh ran a secondhand bookshop on the harbour. In 2019 they took the lease on the Foundry Lane print works, swept out twenty-five years of pigeons, and opened with one exhibition and four shelves of books. The shelves now fill the old bindery. The press still runs once a season.
