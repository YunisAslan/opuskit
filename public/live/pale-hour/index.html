The gallery

Three exhibitions a year in the press hall, a talk every Thursday, and a bookshop in the old bindery.

Pale Hour is for anyone who would rather look at a photograph on a wall than on a screen. Entry is always free. The prints hang where the presses stood, and the books you leave with were chosen by the people who hung them.
