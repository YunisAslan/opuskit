# Media sources — OpusKit kit sample photos

Sample photos used by the kit's section previews (the "product" world's two co-founders, Ines and Karl), not by any
example site. All files are under the Unsplash License, downloaded at Original size on 2026-10-02 with the Unsplash
connector, picked by Claude at the user's request. Each was checked for readable real brand names or logos.
Dropped for that reason: a white-tee man's portrait by Jorge Salvador (iGzHMzRZjRs — small logo on the chest), which
also ruled out pairing it with that photographer's woman (kQjHOnBc6Wg); a smiling man by PodMatch (sNuQ3EhnfYQ — a
company account, captioned with its founder's name); a white-brick man by Vicky Hladynets (_gDoyVv5F4c — the description
names the sitter, a working DJ). None of the three Hane team portraits were reused.

Originals are not kept here (re-download them from the links below). `people/founder-1.jpg` and `people/founder-2.jpg` are 960×1200 (4:5) crops
centred on the face, JPEG q82.
