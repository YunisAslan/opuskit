# KEEPERS — News Grid Product Launch

KEEPERS: Demonstration of a KEEPERS drink in a metal container. A product launch with a news grid direction: workshop manual typography, a electric lime palette, video leading the experience and immersive motion. Primary goal: explore the work.

Complexity: advanced · Recipe id: 1c6a6cfc

## Creative Direction

**Mood:** Direct, Informed, Urgent

**Personality:** Bold headlines, newspaper structure

### Visual principles
- Headlines do the work
- Columns create order
- One strong colour for emphasis

### Do
- Use big, bold headlines
- Organise content in clear columns
- Use rules to separate stories

### Avoid
- Decorative images
- Soft pastels
- Centered paragraphs

### Not the generic AI look
- A cream or beige page ground with a clay/terracotta accent
- A near-black ground with one acid-green or orange accent, or tinted charcoal standing in for black
- Small uppercase, letter-spaced monospace labels above every heading
- Numbered markers (01 / 02) on content that is not a real sequence
- Meta strings joined with middle dots or spaced em dashes, and "→" appended to links
- One italic or coloured accent word inside an otherwise plain headline
- Falling back to Inter, Space Grotesk, Syne or Fraunces instead of the recipe's fonts

### Design principles
- Headlines do the work
- Columns create order
- One strong colour for emphasis
- Pinned, scroll-driven sequences where media and type are choreographed together.

## Why It Works

### Why the visual direction works
Newspaper structure is instantly understood — visitors scan headlines and find what matters fast.

### Why the typography works
Chicago-derived condensed gothic whose small optical size is drawn for labels — structure you can read at a glance, no mono costume.

### Why the palette works
Acid lime as a full page (not a neon accent on black) is pure poster energy — impossible to ignore and very current in music, sport and culture brands.

### Why the layout works
Edge-to-edge media removes the frame of the browser, making the content feel like an environment, not a page.

### Why the motion works
Choreographed sequences create the memorable moments people share — used once or twice, not everywhere. Here, fade & rise reveal and image clip reveal serve the story: give sections a calm entrance so content arrives in reading order.

### Why the chosen assets work
Footage communicates atmosphere and time in a way stills cannot — the visitor feels the place or product before reading.
