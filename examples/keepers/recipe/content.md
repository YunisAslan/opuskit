## Content Direction

- **Tone:** direct, informed, urgent
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Design is a method", "Clarity, at scale", "12 people. 1 studio. 140 projects."
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** No hard sell: the site guides from one piece of work to the next. A single primary action ("Pre-order", "Get it") repeated at the hero and the end, with price nearby.
- **CTA examples:** "See the work", "Next project", "Keep exploring", "Start a project"
- **Content density:** Medium — information-rich but modular
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
