## Content Direction

- **Tone:** light, friendly, functional
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Shaped by hand, fired slowly", "Everyday objects, made to last", "From clay to table"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** No hard sell: the site guides from one piece of work to the next. "Book a table" is always one tap away — sticky on mobile, visible in the navigation on desktop.
- **CTA examples:** "See the work", "Next project", "Keep exploring", "Shop the collection"
- **Content density:** Low to medium
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
