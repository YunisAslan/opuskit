## Content Direction

- **Tone:** clear, stark, confident
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Words, set loudly", "I write things down", "Say less, bigger"
- **Paragraph length:** 1–3 sentences; let headlines carry the page
- **CTA style:** Every page ends with a contact prompt; a real email address is always visible. Newsletter or contact — pick one primary action and let everything else be links.
- **CTA examples:** "Start a project", "Say hello", "Email us", "Read the newsletter"
- **Content density:** Low word count, high visual weight
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
