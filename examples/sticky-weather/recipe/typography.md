## Typography — Stack

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Stack Sans Headline | 600 | clamp(2.75rem, 7.5vw, 6.5rem) | 0.95 | -0.035em | Hero headlines |
| Heading | Stack Sans Headline | 500 | clamp(1.5rem, 2.5vw, 2.1rem) | 1.08 | -0.02em | Section headings |
| Body | Stack Sans Text | 400 | 1.0625rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Stack Sans Text | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, navigation |

Source: Google Fonts (Stack Sans Headline, Stack Sans Text)

**Why this pairing works:** A 2025 open family by the branding studio Koto, with separate headline and text cuts — the agency-made product look without Inter or Geist.
