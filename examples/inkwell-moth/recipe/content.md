## Content Direction

- **Tone:** personal, warm, handmade
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Selected work, 2019—2026", "Design for things that last", "Currently taking on new projects"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** Every page ends with a contact prompt; a real email address is always visible. One quiet, persistent contact link plus a direct email at the end of every case study.
- **CTA examples:** "Start a project", "Say hello", "Email us", "See the work"
- **Content density:** Medium — varies intentionally by section
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
