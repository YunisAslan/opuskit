# Slow Atlas — News Grid Magazine

Slow Atlas: An independent magazine of long-form travel essays — one place, told slowly. A magazine with a news grid direction: newsroom typography, a cherry red palette, typography leading the experience and subtle motion. Primary goal: subscribe.

Complexity: light · Recipe id: swiss-modern

## Creative Direction

**Mood:** Direct, Informed, Urgent

**Personality:** Bold headlines, newspaper structure

### Visual principles
- Headlines do the work
- Columns create order
- One strong colour for emphasis

### Do
- Use big, bold headlines
- Organise content in clear columns
- Use rules to separate stories

### Avoid
- Decorative images
- Soft pastels
- Centered paragraphs

### Not the generic AI look
- A cream or beige page ground with a clay/terracotta accent
- A near-black ground with one acid-green or orange accent, or tinted charcoal standing in for black
- Small uppercase, letter-spaced monospace labels above every heading
- Numbered markers (01 / 02) on content that is not a real sequence
- Meta strings joined with middle dots or spaced em dashes, and "→" appended to links
- One italic or coloured accent word inside an otherwise plain headline
- Falling back to Inter, Space Grotesk, Syne or Fraunces instead of the recipe's fonts

### Design principles
- Headlines do the work
- Columns create order
- One strong colour for emphasis
- Content eases in once as it enters the viewport; media drifts slightly. Nothing loops.

## Why It Works

### Why the visual direction works
Newspaper structure is instantly understood — visitors scan headlines and find what matters fast.

### Why the typography works
Drawn for a Scandinavian newspaper group: punchy grotesk headlines over a serif made for long reading — the modern newsroom look.

### Why the palette works
Cherry red is the fashion colour of the moment: loud, glossy and instantly recognisable. As a full ground it feels like a campaign, not a website.

### Why the layout works
A visible modular grid makes dense content scannable and gives the brand an objective, confident structure.

### Why the motion works
Subtle reveals give rhythm and polish without distracting from reading. Here, fade & rise reveal and line-by-line headline reveal serve the story: give sections a calm entrance so content arrives in reading order.

### Why the chosen assets work
Typography-led sites are fast, accessible and distinctive — the words and the visual become the same thing.
