## Content Direction

- **Tone:** direct, informed, urgent
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "The case for slower design", "This week: three essays", "Read the archive"
- **Paragraph length:** 1–3 sentences; let headlines carry the page
- **CTA style:** An inline sign-up field appears after the strongest content, not as a pop-up. Reading comes first; one quiet newsletter sign-up after each article, never a pop-up.
- **CTA examples:** "Subscribe", "Join the list", "Get the next issue", "Read the latest"
- **Content density:** Medium — information-rich but modular
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
