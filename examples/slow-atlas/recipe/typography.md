## Typography — Newsroom

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Schibsted Grotesk | 800 | clamp(3rem, 8.5vw, 7.5rem) | 0.92 | -0.04em | Hero headlines, big numbers |
| Heading | Schibsted Grotesk | 700 | clamp(1.5rem, 2.6vw, 2.25rem) | 1.05 | -0.02em | Section headings |
| Body | Source Serif 4 | 400 | 1.125rem | 1.6 | 0 | Long reading |
| Utility | Schibsted Grotesk | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, bylines |

Source: Google Fonts (Schibsted Grotesk, Source Serif 4)

**Why this pairing works:** Drawn for a Scandinavian newspaper group: punchy grotesk headlines over a serif made for long reading — the modern newsroom look.
