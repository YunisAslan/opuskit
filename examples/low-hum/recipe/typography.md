## Typography — Soft Seventies

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Gloock | 400 | clamp(3rem, 8vw, 7rem) | 0.98 | -0.02em | Hero headlines |
| Heading | Gloock | 400 | clamp(1.6rem, 2.8vw, 2.4rem) | 1.1 | -0.01em | Section headings |
| Body | Figtree | 400 | 1.0625rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Figtree | 600 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, navigation |

Captions, prices beside pictures and small notes use `type-caption` (Figtree, small, sentence case) — the utility role is for the menu and labels.

Source: Google Fonts (Gloock, Figtree)

**Why this pairing works:** The 70s serif revival: soft, high-contrast curves feel warm and nostalgic, and Figtree keeps the rest clean and easy.
