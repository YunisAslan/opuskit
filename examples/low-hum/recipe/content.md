## Content Direction

- **Tone:** cheeky, quick, confident
- **Voice:** Every heading is a small joke with a point: a problem, then its answer in the same breath. Short sentences. No jargon.
- **Headline style:** Problem? Answer. — “Hard story? Easily told.” “No talk. All pictures.”
- **Headline examples:** "Dinner is served at eight", "A table for you", "Seasonal plates, slow evenings"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** Booking becomes the sticky primary action on mobile and in the navigation. "Book a table" is always one tap away — sticky on mobile, visible in the navigation on desktop.
- **CTA examples:** "Book a table", "Reserve your time", "Check availability", "See the menu"
- **Content density:** Medium — practical info must be complete
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"

## Copy deck — write it before any layout

Write from the owner's own words — the name “Low Hum” and “A listening bar with ten thousand records, a hand-built sound system and small plates until late.” Every headline, line and claim grows from them: name what is really there — what is made, where, when, for whom. The headline and CTA examples are only the register, taken from another site: never reuse them. Anything you must invent (quotes, prices, names, numbers, dates) is marked in the copy deck as a placeholder for the owner to replace.

### Home
Establish mood and promise in seconds; orient the visitor to what this site is and give them a clear next step.

- **Hero:** Establish mood and promise immediately. One headline (≤ 8 words), one supporting line, one action.
- **Intro:** Say what this is and who it is for. 1–2 sentences, specific and concrete.
- **Menu:** Let guests decide before arriving. Dish names, short descriptions, prices.
- **Schedule:** Show what happens when. Every day of the programme, real times, short titles.
- **Reservation:** Convert interest into a booking. Opening hours, group policy, phone number.

### Menu
Let guests decide what they want before they arrive.

- **Menu:** Let guests decide before arriving. Dish names, short descriptions, prices.
- **Gallery:** Let atmosphere speak. 6–10 images with sparse captions.
- **Reservation:** Convert interest into a booking. Opening hours, group policy, phone number.

### Reservations
Convert interest into a booking with minimal friction.

- **Reservation:** Convert interest into a booking. Opening hours, group policy, phone number.
- **Location:** Make visiting easy. Address, hours, transit notes.
- **FAQ:** Answer real objections. 5–8 genuine questions.
