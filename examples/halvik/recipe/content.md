## Content Direction

- **Tone:** clear, friendly, modern
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Meet the new one", "Everything you need, nothing you don’t", "Designed around one idea"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** Product pages, a cart and a short checkout path are prioritised. A single primary action ("Pre-order", "Get it") repeated at the hero and the end, with price nearby.
- **CTA examples:** "Add to bag", "Shop the collection", "Buy now", "Pre-order"
- **Content density:** Medium — information-rich but modular
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
