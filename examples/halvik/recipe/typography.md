## Typography — Wide Spec

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Hubot Sans | 800 | clamp(2.5rem, 7vw, 6rem) | 0.95 | -0.02em · width 125% | Hero statements |
| Heading | Hubot Sans | 700 | clamp(1.4rem, 2.4vw, 2rem) | 1.1 | -0.01em · width 112.5% | Section headings |
| Body | IBM Plex Sans | 400 | 1.0625rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs, specs |
| Utility | IBM Plex Sans | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 · width 87.5% | Labels, navigation, data |

Source: Google Fonts (Hubot Sans, IBM Plex Sans)

**Why this pairing works:** GitHub's wide Hubot for headlines over IBM Plex for reading — two corporate open families that real tech brands run on (KPR pairs a wide grotesk with Plex). Width, not colour, makes the hierarchy.
