# Media sources — Pale Hour

Found and picked by Claude through the Unsplash connector on 2026-10-08, following the shot list in `recipe/media.md`; each photo replaces a temporary picture file for file (same name, same pixel size, JPEG), and no code was changed. Every final was cropped to its slot around a chosen focal point, resized, and given one shared grade with identical parameters (a touch of S-curve contrast, slightly lifted blacks and softened whites, colour eased to 93%, a slight cool-neutral balance), then saved as JPEG q82 progressive. Before the grade, the artwork of *The Quiet Rooms*, the director and the four team portraits were converted to black and white, and *Salt Roads* to a warm-grey monochrome (platinum/palladium look). All photos are under the Unsplash License (free to use, no attribution required); the photographers are credited here anyway. Originals (up to 4000 px) are in `media-src/originals/`, and `media-src/contact-sheet.jpg` shows all 23 finals.
