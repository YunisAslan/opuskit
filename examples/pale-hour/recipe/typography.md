## Typography — Cut Glass

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Prata | 400 | clamp(3rem, 8.5vw, 8rem) | 0.95 | -0.025em | Hero lines, collection and property names — never italic (Prata has none) |
| Heading | Prata | 400 | clamp(1.6rem, 2.8vw, 2.5rem) | 1.08 | -0.01em | Section headings |
| Body | Public Sans | 400 | 1rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs, product details |
| Utility | Public Sans | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Navigation, prices, labels |

Captions, prices beside pictures and small notes use `type-caption` (Public Sans, small, sentence case) — the utility role is for the menu and labels.

Source: Google Fonts (Prata, Public Sans)

**Why this pairing works:** The Editorial New / Canela register that award sites license (Hilden Kaira, Normal is Boring) — and that templates fake with Playfair Display (8 of our 225 sites). Prata's wedge serifs and Didone contrast read sharper and less familiar; Public Sans (Franklin-derived, USWDS) is the plain workhorse beside it, as Neue Montreal is on the real thing.
