## Content Direction

- **Tone:** exact, factual
- **Voice:** Specific numbers, real specs, no superlatives.
- **Headline style:** Declarative facts
- **Headline examples:** "Learn lettering in six weeks", "Next cohort starts 3 March", "What you’ll make"
- **Paragraph length:** 1–3 sentences; let headlines carry the page
- **CTA style:** Requirements and deadlines come first, then a short step-by-step application form. Enrol with the price and start date beside the button; a free preview lesson is the secondary action.
- **CTA examples:** "Apply now", "Enrol", "Check eligibility", "Enrol now"
- **Content density:** Medium — information-rich but modular
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"

## Copy deck — write it before any layout

Write from the owner's own words — the name “Raster School” and “A six-week evening course in typographic design: grids, lettering and a poster of your own at the end. Twelve seats a cohort, in our studio or online.” Every headline, line and claim grows from them: name what is really there — what is made, where, when, for whom. The headline and CTA examples are only the register, taken from another site: never reuse them. Anything you must invent (quotes, prices, names, numbers, dates) is marked in the copy deck as a placeholder for the owner to replace.

### Home
Establish mood and promise in seconds; orient the visitor to what this site is and give them a clear next step.

- **Hero:** Establish mood and promise immediately. One headline (≤ 8 words), one supporting line, one action.
- **Manifesto:** State the point of view boldly. 1–3 sentences that could only be yours.
- **Process:** Show what happens, step by step. Step name, 1–2 sentences, and how long it takes where that matters.
- **Team:** Show the people behind it. 3–8 people: real photo, name, role, one sentence.
- **Testimonials:** Let real people vouch for the work. 3–4 real quotes, each with a name and who they are to this place (client, guest, customer, member).
- **Pricing:** Remove the final uncertainty. Plan name, price, what's included.
- **Schedule:** Show what happens when. Every day of the programme, real times, short titles.
- **FAQ:** Answer real objections. 5–8 genuine questions.
- **Closing CTA:** End with one clear next step. Headline in brand voice, one action, and the real ways to reach you (email, and phone or address where they exist).
