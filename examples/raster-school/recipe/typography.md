## Typography — Grid Discipline

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Zalando Sans | 800 | clamp(3rem, 9vw, 8.5rem) | 0.9 | -0.045em | Hero, big numbers |
| Heading | Zalando Sans | 700 | clamp(1.5rem, 2.5vw, 2.25rem) | 1.05 | -0.02em | Section headings |
| Body | Zalando Sans | 400 | 1rem | 1.5 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Zalando Sans | 600 | 0.8125rem | 1.3 | 0 | Labels, captions — tabular figures |

Captions, prices beside pictures and small notes use `type-caption` (Zalando Sans, small, sentence case) — the utility role is for the menu and labels.

Source: Google Fonts (Zalando Sans)

**Why this pairing works:** Swiss rigour from one 2025 neo-grotesk with a width axis: hierarchy by weight and width only, without the Helvetica/Inter look.
