## Typography — Soft Wedge

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Petrona | 300 | clamp(3rem, 7.5vw, 6.75rem) | 0.98 | -0.03em | Hero lines — italic for whole phrases |
| Heading | Petrona | 400 | clamp(1.6rem, 2.8vw, 2.4rem) | 1.1 | -0.015em | Section headings |
| Body | Public Sans | 400 | 1.0625rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Public Sans | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, navigation |

Source: Google Fonts (Petrona, Public Sans)

**Why this pairing works:** Reckless Neue, Signifier and Teodor are the serifs of 2026 award sites (Colonia Zacamil, Mosby Files, Partizan): low-contrast, light, a little soft at the joins. Petrona (100–900 + italics) is the closest free family in that register; Public Sans keeps the interface plain.
