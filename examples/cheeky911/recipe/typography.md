## Typography — Stretch Test

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Anybody | 800 | clamp(3rem, 10vw, 9rem) | 0.9 | -0.02em · width 140% | Hero, project titles — vary width per line |
| Heading | Anybody | 700 | clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.5rem) | 1.05 | -0.01em | Section headings |
| Body | Spectral | 400 | 1.125rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Spectral | 400 italic | 0.9375rem | 1.4 | 0 | Captions, index labels in italic |

Source: Google Fonts (Anybody, Spectral)

**Why this pairing works:** Width becomes an art-direction axis inside one file, while Spectral — a screen-first text serif — keeps long copy steady.
