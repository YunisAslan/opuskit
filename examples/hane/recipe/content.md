## Content Direction

- **Tone:** Quiet, precise, unhurried
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Gentle care, open late", "Treatments for the whole family", "Meet your dentist"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** Booking becomes the sticky primary action on mobile and in the navigation. "Book an appointment" sits in the header and after every treatment; the phone number is always visible.
- **CTA examples:** "Book a table", "Reserve your time", "Check availability", "Book an appointment"
- **Content density:** Very low — one idea per screen, 40–80 words per section
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
