## Content Direction

- **Tone:** dramatic, dark, rebellious
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "The new collection", "Cut close, worn loose", "Made in small runs"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** Product pages, a cart and a short checkout path are prioritised. Understated text links — "Discover the collection", "Shop the look" — never loud buttons.
- **CTA examples:** "Add to bag", "Shop the collection", "Buy now", "Discover the collection"
- **Content density:** Low — media carries meaning; text sets context in 1–2 lines
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
