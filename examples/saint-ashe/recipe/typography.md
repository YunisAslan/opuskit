## Typography — New Gothic

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Grenze Gotisch | 700 | clamp(3rem, 9vw, 8rem) | 0.9 | 0 | Hero words, titles |
| Heading | Grenze Gotisch | 600 | clamp(1.6rem, 3vw, 2.5rem) | 1 | 0 | Section headings |
| Body | Work Sans | 400 | 1rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Work Sans | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, navigation |

Source: Google Fonts (Grenze Gotisch, Work Sans)

**Why this pairing works:** Blackletter is back in music, streetwear and tattoo culture. A condensed modern gothic gives drama; Work Sans keeps everything else readable.
