## Content Direction

- **Tone:** loud, honest, energetic
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Every village deserves a library", "312 libraries built so far", "Where your gift goes"
- **Paragraph length:** 1–3 sentences; let headlines carry the page
- **CTA style:** A donate button with preset amounts, each showing what it achieves. Donate is the one primary action, with what each amount achieves; volunteering is the secondary one.
- **CTA examples:** "Donate", "Become a member", "Support our work", "Volunteer with us"
- **Content density:** High but structured — modules keep it scannable
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
