## Content Direction

- **Tone:** exact, quiet, competent
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Learn lettering in six weeks", "Next cohort starts 3 March", "What you’ll make"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** One primary "start" action in the hero and the end; pricing stays reachable. Enrol with the price and start date beside the button; a free preview lesson is the secondary action.
- **CTA examples:** "Start free", "Get started", "Book a demo", "Enrol now"
- **Content density:** Medium-high, structured in modules
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
