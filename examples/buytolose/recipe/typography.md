## Typography — Round Future

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Unbounded | 700 | clamp(2.5rem, 7vw, 6rem) | 0.95 | -0.03em | Hero headlines |
| Heading | Unbounded | 600 | clamp(1.4rem, 2.4vw, 2rem) | 1.1 | -0.01em | Section headings |
| Body | Onest | 400 | 1rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Onest | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, navigation |

Source: Google Fonts (Unbounded, Onest)

**Why this pairing works:** Wide, rounded geometry is the look of new tech and Web3-era brands — bold and friendly — with Onest as a calm reading partner.
