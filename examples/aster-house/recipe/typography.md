## Typography — Quiet Page

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Literata | 300 | clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem) | 1.05 | -0.02em | Hero lines, chapter titles |
| Heading | Literata | 500 | clamp(1.5rem, 2.5vw, 2.1rem) | 1.15 | -0.01em | Section headings |
| Body | Literata | 400 | 1.125rem | 1.6 | 0 | Long-form reading |
| Utility | Host Grotesk | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Navigation, captions, labels — sentence case |

Source: Google Fonts (Literata, Host Grotesk)

**Why this pairing works:** Literata was drawn for long reading and has a real optical-size axis, so light display sizes stay crisp. One small grotesk keeps the interface apart from the prose.
