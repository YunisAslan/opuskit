## Typography — Poster Caps

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Bayon | 400 | clamp(4rem, 13vw, 12rem) | 0.85 | 0 (uppercase) | Hero words — big, uppercase, few |
| Heading | Bayon | 400 | clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3.25rem) | 0.95 | 0.01em (uppercase) | Section headings |
| Body | Reddit Sans | 400 | 1.0625rem | 1.55 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Reddit Sans | 600 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, navigation |

Source: Google Fonts (Bayon, Reddit Sans)

**Why this pairing works:** Tall, tightly-set capitals shout like a flyer while a plain grotesk does the talking (Dontboardme pairs Bayon with Neue Montreal). Fresher than Bebas or Anton, which every template uses.
