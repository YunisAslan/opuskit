## Content Direction

- **Tone:** Witty, opinionated, direct
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "We make brands people remember", "Strategy, design, launch", "Work that moved the numbers"
- **Paragraph length:** 1–3 sentences; let headlines carry the page
- **CTA style:** Every page ends with a contact prompt; a real email address is always visible. "Start a project" in the navigation and a large closing contact section with a real email address.
- **CTA examples:** "Start a project", "Say hello", "Email us", "See case studies"
- **Content density:** Low word count, high visual weight
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
