## Typography — Bubble Pop

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Bagel Fat One | 400 | clamp(3rem, 10vw, 9rem) | 0.9 | -0.01em | Hero words, stickers |
| Heading | Epilogue | 800 | clamp(1.5rem, 2.8vw, 2.4rem) | 1.05 | -0.02em | Section headings |
| Body | Epilogue | 400 | 1.0625rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Epilogue | 600 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, navigation |

Captions, prices beside pictures and small notes use `type-caption` (Epilogue, small, sentence case) — the utility role is for the menu and labels.

Source: Google Fonts (Bagel Fat One, Epilogue)

**Why this pairing works:** Chunky, inflated letters are the fun-brand trend of drinks, snacks and apps. Epilogue is bold enough to keep up but calm enough to read.
