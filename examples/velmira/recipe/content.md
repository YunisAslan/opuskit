## Content Direction

- **Tone:** calm, luminous, gentle
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Nine rooms above the olive groves", "Stay a while", "Slow mornings, long dinners"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** Booking becomes the sticky primary action on mobile and in the navigation. "Check availability" is always one tap away, with dates and guests picked right there.
- **CTA examples:** "Book a table", "Reserve your time", "Check availability", "See the rooms"
- **Content density:** Very low — one idea per screen, 40–80 words per section
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
