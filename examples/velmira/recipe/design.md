# Velmira — Ethereal Hotel Site

Velmira: Nine rooms and a bathhouse on a lake in the Gabala hills: warm water, cold air, long quiet mornings. A hotel site with a ethereal direction: kalnia couture typography, a midnight chapters palette, video leading the experience and subtle motion. Primary goal: book or reserve.

Complexity: moderate · Recipe id: japanese-quiet-minimal

## The Big Idea — One thing guides the scroll

Something from the first screen travels with visitors down the page. It suits a hotel site with a Ethereal look at subtle movement.

Build the whole site around this one idea. Every page gets one moment that serves it, and nothing else competes with that moment.

- **What guides the scroll:** Pick one element from the first screen — the product, the logo mark, a drawn line or a simple shape in the accent colour — and let it leave the hero and travel down the page with the visitor, turning up beside each chapter.
- **How each chapter opens:** Each chapter opens where the motif comes to rest: the motif changes pose or size there, and the chapter title sits next to it.
- **The moment people remember:** The motif’s arrival in the chapter that matters most (the work, the product, the menu) — it lands, and the content builds around it.
- **How the site ends:** The motif comes to rest in the footer, beside the brand name: the journey visibly ends.

It reaches the pages through these signature moments (details in the motion recipe): One shape travels down the page — Home — Intro; A footer worth reaching — Footer.

## Creative Direction

**Mood:** Calm, Luminous, Gentle

**Personality:** Soft light, pearl colours, things that float

### Visual principles
- Light over colour
- Nothing heavy, nothing hard
- Slow, floating motion

### Do
- Soft radial glows behind key content
- Frosted glass panels over light imagery
- Hairline display type with generous spacing

### Avoid
- Low-contrast text on pale glows
- Hard shadows
- Busy imagery

### Not the generic AI look
- A cream or beige page ground with a clay/terracotta accent
- A near-black ground with one acid-green or orange accent, or tinted charcoal standing in for black
- Small uppercase, letter-spaced monospace labels above every heading
- Numbered markers (01 / 02) on content that is not a real sequence
- Meta strings joined with middle dots or spaced em dashes, and "→" appended to links
- One italic or coloured accent word inside an otherwise plain headline
- Falling back to Inter, Space Grotesk, Syne or Fraunces instead of the recipe's fonts

### Design principles
- Light over colour
- Nothing heavy, nothing hard
- Slow, floating motion
- Content eases in once as it enters the viewport; media drifts slightly. Nothing loops.

## Award checklist

What separates an award-winning site from a good template (from a study of 12 Awwwards sites). Check every page against it.

- One idea: everything serves “One thing guides the scroll”. A part that doesn’t serve it gets quieter, not louder.
- One unforgettable moment per page — and only one. Everything else on that page supports it.
- Type scale contrast: the biggest Kalnia size is at least 6× the body size on desktop, labels stay small (11–14 px, SUSE), and nothing in between competes.
- Motion choreography: one thing moves at a time; each arrival enters, holds and leaves; staggers of 40–80 ms; the same one or two easings everywhere.
- The first seconds: the first screen is complete and readable before anything animates.
- Mobile is its own composition: headlines re-broken by hand, media re-cropped, pinned and hover effects replaced by their mobile versions — never a squeezed desktop.
- The ending is designed: the footer (Signature columns) is a moment — the motif comes to rest in the footer, beside the brand name: the journey visibly ends.
- Craft details: text selection in the accent colour, a favicon from the logo, designed focus states, no layout shift, real copy everywhere, a 404 page in the same voice.
- Smooth is part of the effect: 60 fps on a mid-range laptop; animate transform and opacity only; nothing runs off-screen.

## Why It Works

### Why the visual direction works
Feels serene and premium — for wellness, beauty, skincare, spas, weddings and meditation.

### Why the typography works
A high-contrast display serif that widens on an axis — the Canela / Editorial New register with more warmth — over SUSE, a calm geometric sans from 2024.

### Why the palette works
A deep ultraviolet, bluer than plum and brighter than navy, is a night sky that is plainly a colour (Inside the Head). Coral pink warms it without neon.

### Why the layout works
Edge-to-edge media removes the frame of the browser, making the content feel like an environment, not a page.

### Why the motion works
Subtle reveals give rhythm and polish without distracting from reading. Here, fade & rise reveal and image clip reveal serve the story: give sections a calm entrance so content arrives in reading order.

### Why the chosen assets work
Footage communicates atmosphere and time in a way stills cannot — the visitor feels the place or product before reading.
