## Typography — Letterpress Modern

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Hedvig Letters Serif | 400 | clamp(2.75rem, 6.5vw, 5.75rem) | 1.02 | -0.02em | Hero headlines |
| Heading | Hedvig Letters Serif | 400 | clamp(1.5rem, 2.6vw, 2.2rem) | 1.15 | -0.01em | Section headings |
| Body | Hedvig Letters Sans | 400 | 1.0625rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Hedvig Letters Sans | 400 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, navigation |

Captions, prices beside pictures and small notes use `type-caption` (Hedvig Letters Sans, small, sentence case) — the utility role is for the menu and labels.

Source: Google Fonts (Hedvig Letters Serif, Hedvig Letters Sans)

**Why this pairing works:** A serif and sans drawn as one family, so headline and text belong together — the quiet, well-made editorial look.
