## Content Direction

- **Tone:** Precise, calm, evidence-based
- **Voice:** Plain, specific, confident.
- **Headline style:** Short, specific statements
- **Headline examples:** "Infrastructure you can see", "Latency, measured", "Signal over noise"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** One primary "start" action in the hero and the end; pricing stays reachable. Primary "Start free" + secondary "See how it works"; pricing reachable from the navigation.
- **CTA examples:** "Start free", "Get started", "Book a demo", "Start building"
- **Content density:** Medium-high, structured in modules
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
