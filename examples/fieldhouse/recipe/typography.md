## Typography — Moonlit Italic

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Cormorant | 500 italic | clamp(3.25rem, 9vw, 8rem) | 0.95 | -0.02em | Hero — whole phrases in italic |
| Heading | Cormorant | 600 | clamp(1.75rem, 3vw, 2.5rem) | 1.1 | -0.01em | Section headings |
| Body | Karla | 400 | 1rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Karla | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, navigation |

Source: Google Fonts (Cormorant, Karla)

**Why this pairing works:** Graceful Garamond italics at huge sizes feel romantic and expensive; Karla is a slightly quirky sans that keeps it from feeling stiff.
