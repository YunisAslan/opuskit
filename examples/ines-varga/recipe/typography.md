## Typography — Sketchbook

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Caveat Brush | 400 | clamp(3rem, 9vw, 7.5rem) | 0.95 | 0 | Hero statements |
| Heading | Caveat | 700 | clamp(1.6rem, 3vw, 2.4rem) | 1.05 | 0 | Section headings |
| Body | Winky Sans | 400 | 1.1875rem | 1.55 | 0.01em | Paragraphs |
| Utility | Winky Sans | 500 | 0.9375rem | 1.3 | 0.02em | Labels, navigation |

Captions, prices beside pictures and small notes use `type-caption` (Winky Sans, small, sentence case) — the utility role is for the menu and labels.

Source: Google Fonts (Caveat Brush, Caveat, Winky Sans)

**Why this pairing works:** Everything looks written by one hand: a brush-marker title, quick notes, and a friendly plain sans for reading — handwriting for paragraphs was the dated part.
