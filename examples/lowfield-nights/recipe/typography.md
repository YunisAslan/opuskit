## Typography — High and Low

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Newsreader | 700 | clamp(3rem, 8vw, 7rem) | 0.95 | -0.025em | Hero — set whole phrases in 200 italic, never a single accent word |
| Heading | Newsreader | 600 | clamp(1.75rem, 3vw, 2.5rem) | 1.1 | -0.01em | Section headings |
| Body | Albert Sans | 400 | 1.0625rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs, UI copy |
| Utility | Albert Sans | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Captions, labels |

Source: Google Fonts (Newsreader, Albert Sans)

**Why this pairing works:** Contrast comes from weight inside the serif itself — heavy roman against light italic phrases — so the voice changes without a second display face.
