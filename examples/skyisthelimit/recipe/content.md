## Content Direction

- **Tone:** Confident, curious, playful
- **Voice:** Describe what becomes possible, concretely.
- **Headline style:** Short, forward statements
- **Headline examples:** "We make things look like themselves", "Direction, not decoration", "Ideas with edges"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** Optional. If present, it should feel like part of the piece, not a conversion device.
- **CTA examples:** "Start something", "See the archive"
- **Content density:** Medium — varies intentionally by section
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"
