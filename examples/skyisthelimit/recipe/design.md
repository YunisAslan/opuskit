# Future Art Direction Experiment

A experiment with a art direction direction: experimental typography, a high contrast palette, 3d leading the experience and dynamic motion.

Complexity: advanced · Recipe id: e9f8953e

## Creative Direction

**Mood:** Expressive, Unexpected, Crafted

**Personality:** A creative director who breaks rules knowingly.

### Visual principles
- Every section is composed, never templated
- Break the grid on purpose, keep one anchor per section
- Transitions are part of the concept
- One surprising interaction per page, not ten

### Do
- Overlap type and media with deliberate crops
- Use cobalt as the studio's signature on hover and index
- Vary section spacing to create rhythm
- Keep navigation conventional so people never get lost

### Avoid
- Random effects without a concept
- Unreadable text over images
- Custom cursors that hide the real cursor
- Scroll hijacking

## Why It Works

### Why the visual direction works
An art-directed site proves creative capability directly — prospective clients experience the studio's thinking instead of reading about it.

### Why the typography works
Syne was drawn for an art centre — its widening weights feel expressive; DM Sans and DM Mono stay neutral and legible around it.

### Why the palette works
Flat, saturated blocks on a light ground create instant energy; ink borders make the structure part of the aesthetic.

### Why the layout works
Breaking expectations makes a site memorable — as long as one clear anchor per section keeps it usable.

### Why the motion works
Scroll-linked motion makes the visitor feel in control of the story. Here, fade & rise reveal and line-by-line headline reveal serve the story: give sections a calm entrance so content arrives in reading order.

### Why the chosen assets work
Interactive depth creates presence and invites exploration — when restrained, it reads as technical mastery.
