## Typography — Experimental

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Syne | 700 | clamp(3rem, 9vw, 9rem) | 0.9 | -0.03em | Hero, project titles |
| Heading | Syne | 600 | clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.5rem) | 1.05 | -0.02em | Section headings |
| Body | DM Sans | 400 | 1rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Utility | DM Mono | 400 | 0.75rem | 1.4 | 0.04em (uppercase) | Labels, index numbers |

Source: Google Fonts (Syne, DM Sans, DM Mono)

**Why this pairing works:** Syne was drawn for an art centre — its widening weights feel expressive; DM Sans and DM Mono stay neutral and legible around it.
