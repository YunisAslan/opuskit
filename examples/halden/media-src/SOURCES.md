web files and posters were made from it by the package's own `scripts/prepare-video.sh` (no upscale). The photos were
found and picked by Claude through the Unsplash connector (2026-10-07), by the asset layer's shot list, and saved over
the build's temporary pictures file for file (no code changed). No brand names or logos. Unsplash licence:
free for commercial use, no attribution required — credited here anyway. Originals (up to 4000 px) in this folder;
graded copies, cropped to each asset key's exact size, in `public/media/`.
