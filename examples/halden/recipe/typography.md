## Typography — Opening Credits

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Imbue | 300 | clamp(4.5rem, 14vw, 13rem) | 0.85 | -0.01em | Hero words, film titles |
| Heading | Imbue | 500 | clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3.5rem) | 0.95 | 0 | Chapter headings |
| Body | Hanken Grotesk | 400 | 1rem | 1.6 | 0 | Paragraphs, captions under footage |
| Utility | Hanken Grotesk | 500 | 0.8125rem | 1.4 | 0 | Labels, time-codes |

Source: Google Fonts (Imbue, Hanken Grotesk)

**Why this pairing works:** Condensed high contrast fills the screen like a film title without spending its width; Hanken stays out of the way under footage.
