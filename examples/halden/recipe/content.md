## Content Direction

- **Tone:** intriguing, withholding
- **Voice:** Say less than you know. Let media finish the sentence.
- **Headline style:** Fragments and single words
- **Headline examples:** "Gentle care, open late", "Treatments for the whole family", "Meet your dentist"
- **Paragraph length:** 2–4 sentences (40–80 words); never more than 65 characters per line
- **CTA style:** Booking becomes the sticky primary action on mobile and in the navigation. "Book an appointment" sits in the header and after every treatment; the phone number is always visible.
- **CTA examples:** "Book a table", "Reserve your time", "Check availability", "Book an appointment"
- **Content density:** Low — media carries meaning; text sets context in 1–2 lines
- **Words to avoid:** "Elevate your brand", "The future of…", "Seamless experiences", "Unlock your potential", "Built for modern teams", "Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "World-class"

## Copy deck — write it before any layout

Write from the owner's own words — the name “Halden” and “A wood-fired sauna and cold-sea bathhouse on a northern coast: heat, salt water and the long quiet in between.” Every headline, line and claim grows from them: name what is really there — what is made, where, when, for whom. The headline and CTA examples are only the register, taken from another site: never reuse them. Anything you must invent (quotes, prices, names, numbers, dates) is marked in the copy deck as a placeholder for the owner to replace.

### Home
Establish mood and promise in seconds; orient the visitor to what this site is and give them a clear next step.

- **Hero:** Establish mood and promise immediately. One headline (≤ 8 words), one supporting line, one action.
- **Services:** Make offers clear. 4–6 services, each with a one-line description.
- **How It Works:** Show the workflow in steps. 3–4 steps, one sentence each.
- **Gallery:** Let atmosphere speak. 6–10 images with sparse captions.
- **Testimonials:** Let real people vouch for the work. 3–4 real quotes, each with a name and role.
- **Pricing:** Remove the final uncertainty. Plan name, price, what's included.
- **Location:** Make visiting easy. Address, hours, transit notes.
- **Reservation:** Convert interest into a booking. Opening hours, group policy, phone number.
