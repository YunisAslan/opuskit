# Part 4 — Colour

**Source:** the palettes of the 93 Sites of the Day from part 1 (`data/sotd.json`), compared on 2026-10-11 with
OpusKit's 43 palettes in OKLCH.

## What the data says

- **Dark grounds win:** of the sites that list a neutral, 72% list a near-black (52 against 20). OpusKit: 20 of 43
  palettes are dark (47%).
- **Near-black + one signal** is the most common pair. The signals, by hue: red-orange 5 (#FF4421, #FF5121, #FE5B2A,
  #FA4C14, #FF7511), electric ultramarine 5 (#0004EB, #03049C, #3932DC, #1500E1, #0016CB), deep red 3, acid lime 2,
  mint on dark teal 1. Light sites lean on a navy or cobalt instead.
- Part 1 already showed the rest: palettes are tiny (two colours on 73 of 88), and 42% are greyscale only — White Box
  came from that.

## What moved into OpusKit

- **Ultramarine Night** — blue-black, cool white, electric ultramarine (the accent is a field, never text); on
  Immersive Portfolio, Digital Futurism and Technical Minimal; offered for SaaS and studios.
- **Deep Teal** — dark teal, sea white, mint; on Technical Minimal, Coastal Calm and Organic Modern; offered for
  nonprofits and clinics. Its ground was pushed to a more committed teal (#083535) — at #0F2626 it sat too close to
  Espresso (brown + mint) for check.ts.

Not taken: black + red-orange (Charcoal Signal has it), acid lime on black (the user saw acid accents as a tell), a
second deep red (too close to Charcoal Signal by ground and accent).
