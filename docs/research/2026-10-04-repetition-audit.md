# Repetition audit: where the kit repeats itself

2026-10-04 · read-only audit of sections, pieces, patterns, taxonomy, the engine's output and the 15 built examples.

## Summary

The owner is right, and forms are only one case of it. Sites feel alike for **four structural reasons**. None of them is "not enough options":

1. **Every ready section has the same frame.** 39 of 40 sections hardcode `max-w-[1440px]`. 29 use the same `px-5 py-24 md:px-10 md:py-32`. 19 are a `md:grid-cols-12` split. 12 are hairline-ruled lists. Only 2 content sections ever leave the page ground (`ContactCta inverse`, `ColourChapters`). The recipe's layout choice is never carried into the shipped code: *balanced* (1200 px, centred headings), *asymmetric* (5vw margins) and *full-bleed* all produce the same section code. So every site is one long same-coloured strip of left-aligned h2 → content blocks, whatever the palette.
2. **The engine fills the same defaults whatever the owner picks.** Footer defaults to `signature` for every look (`engine.ts:723`, with its own ponytail note). The voice is "Plain, specific, confident." in **14 of 15** example packages, because only 1 of 41 directions has a `voice` (`taxonomy.ts:697`) and the kit has no other way to set `characters`. `SectionHeader` ("Index number, label, heading") is forced into every recipe (`engine.ts:744`), which contradicts two of our own `GENERIC_TELLS`. Fade-rise plus line-reveal plus state-feedback ship on every site at subtle or above.
3. **The big-idea layer has six concepts, and they share their moments.** In the kit, concepts alone pick a site's signatures (`plan.ts:357` writes `signatures: []`). `giant-word-chapters` is in 2 of 6 concepts, and `footer-moment` is in 3 of 6. 4 of 6 concept endings set the brand name huge, and so do the `wordmark` footer and the `reveal-footer` signature. Result: 5 of 15 examples have giant cropped chapter words, 7 built a "Chapter…" component, and 4 built the same Motif/MotifTraveller.
4. **The kit leaves the hard, repeated parts unwritten.** No section ships a form. `ContactCta` has none, even though the contact page is `contact-cta + location/faq`. So the 15 examples hand-wrote **16 form files (~1,400 lines)**: BookingForm ×4, ContactForm ×4, QuestionForm ×2, and more. Each one re-solves mailto + Calendar/Popover/Select + toast. Other hand-written repeats: `MediaAsset` (10 sites), `Lines` line-reveal (7), reveal observers (all 15), `StatusLine` (4), and pinned "proof/stops/film" sequences (5 sites, 5 different implementations).

Most fixes are **merges, defaults and tokens, not new things**. The only addition I'd argue for is one pinned-stage primitive, because it replaces five hand-built copies.

Numbers used below come from: the 40 `src/sections/*.tsx`; a script over `purposes` (144 pages, 77 with sections); the 15 example specs composed through `composeRecipe` + `claudeCodeAdapter`, then diffed line by line (174 lines of ≥ 40 characters are identical in all 15 packages); greps over `examples/*/src`; and a contact sheet of the 15 screenshot cards.

## Ranked: the 15 highest-value changes for variety and beauty

1. **Layout tokens into the section code.** Write `--container`, `--gutter`, `--section-y` and `--ratio-card` from the chosen layout into `tokens.css`, and replace the hardcoded `max-w-[1440px] px-5 md:px-10 py-24 md:py-32` / `aspect-[4/5]` in all 40 sections. Balanced becomes a centred 1200 px column, asymmetric gets 5vw margins with uneven spacing, editorial gets 3:4 covers. *Biggest single source of sameness. M.*
2. **One `tone` prop on every section** (`ground | surface | inverse | chapter`, one wrapper class). The engine alternates tones per page (proof on surface, closing on inverse, offers in chapter colours), so pages get rhythm instead of one strip. *S–M.*
3. **Merge Reservation, Donate and Newsletter, and add the missing contact form, into one "Ask" section** with four layouts: *book* (facts + form card), *write* (open full-width form, big labels, no card; this becomes the contact page's first section), *subscribe* (inline one-line field in an inverse band), *give* (the gift rows are the toggle group). It removes the identical form-card split and the 15× hand-built forms. *M.*
4. **Give every direction a `voice`** (one of the 9 existing characters). It ends "Plain, specific, confident." in 14 of 15 packages. Data only. *S.*
5. **Recommend the footer per look** (`recommendedFooter` beside `recommendedNav`) instead of `signature` for all, and drop a trailing contact-cta when the footer is `contact`. *S.*
6. **Give each concept its own ending.** Only `giant-chapters` (and the wordmark footer) set the brand name huge; `playful-way-in` ends on an interaction, `loud-and-quiet` ends quiet, `one-guide` ends on the motif. Fold `reveal-footer` into `footer-moment`. *S.*
7. **Make `loud-and-quiet` photo-led** (`zoom-into-image` + curtain covers instead of `giant-word-chapters`), and narrow `giant-word-chapters.sections` to five chapter openers. Giant cropped words then come from one concept, not five sites. *S.*
8. **Remove `SectionHeader` from the forced components** and the chrome rows (Navigation/Footer/Hero/CTA) from the components table. They contradict the chosen menu/footer and our own generic tells, and they drive the "01 / // label" chrome. *S.*
9. **Per-family reveal vocabulary** instead of fade-rise + line-reveal everywhere (quiet: opacity; editorial: clip; bold/raw: cut; playful: spring; technical: decode). Drop `line-reveal` whenever a headline behaviour is picked. *M.*
10. **Merge Process + HowItWorks into "Steps"** with *columns / cards with pictures / vertical rail* variants. Merge **Clients + Integrations** into "Name wall", and redesign **Press** as one big pull quote plus a quiet line, so the three cell grids become one. *S.*
11. **Merge Stats + Trust ("Facts row": figures/promises), Menu + Schedule (one listing, `price | time`, with an optional day-strip) and Intro + Manifesto ("Statement": heading/display).** Fewer near-equal choices, and the same visuals stop appearing under different names. *S.*
