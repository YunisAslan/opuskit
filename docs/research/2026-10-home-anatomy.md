Real length varies by kind; OpusKit sits 5–8 everywhere.
- Short in reality: portfolio 1–4 (median 2), experiment 0–3, restaurant/studio/fashion 2–6.
- Long in reality: SaaS/product/clinic/nonprofit/real-estate/e-com/course 7–12; magazines/conferences 10–15.
