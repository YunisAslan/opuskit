# RALPH&LAUREN — Swiss Modern Event Site

A event site with a swiss modern direction: loud and clear typography, a signal white palette, video leading the experience and immersive motion. Primary goal: book or reserve.

Complexity: advanced · Recipe id: 0db65967

## Creative Direction

**Mood:** Rational, Direct, Timeless

**Personality:** An engineer with impeccable taste.

### Visual principles
- The grid is the brand
- Hierarchy through size, weight and position only
- Flush-left, ragged-right, always
- One signal color, used for meaning

### Do
- Number only the process steps — sequence, not decoration
- Use hairline rules to separate modules
- Set big numbers for facts (years, projects, people)
- Keep images rectangular and grid-snapped

### Avoid
- Rounded corners and soft shadows
- Centred text
- Decorative imagery
- Motion that doesn't explain anything

### Not the generic AI look
- A cream or beige page ground with a clay/terracotta accent
- A near-black ground with one acid-green or orange accent, or tinted charcoal standing in for black
- Small uppercase, letter-spaced monospace labels above every heading
- Numbered markers (01 / 02) on content that is not a real sequence
- Meta strings joined with middle dots or spaced em dashes, and "→" appended to links
- One italic or coloured accent word inside an otherwise plain headline
- Falling back to Inter, Space Grotesk, Syne or Fraunces instead of the recipe's fonts

## Why It Works

### Why the visual direction works
The International Typographic Style was designed to communicate clearly and objectively. On the web it makes dense information feel trustworthy and positions the agency as rigorous.

### Why the typography works
One superfamily from wide to narrow, so proportion does the shouting instead of colour or decoration.

### Why the palette works
A real white and a real black are rarer than they look — most sites settle for cream and charcoal. With one saturated red the page reads objective and certain.

### Why the layout works
A visible modular grid makes dense content scannable and gives the brand an objective, confident structure.

### Why the motion works
Choreographed sequences create the memorable moments people share — used once or twice, not everywhere. Here, fade & rise reveal and image clip reveal serve the story: give sections a calm entrance so content arrives in reading order.

### Why the chosen assets work
Footage communicates atmosphere and time in a way stills cannot — the visitor feels the place or product before reading.
